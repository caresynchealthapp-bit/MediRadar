Group Project by Team Codecrafters

Bridging Patients & Licensed Pharmacies in Real Time

MediRadar is a dynamic digital healthcare and pharmacy stock discovery platform engineered by Team Codecrafters. Our mission is to eliminate the stress of traveling from pharmacy to pharmacy searching for critical medications by connecting patients directly to live Goods Received Note (GRN) pharmacy inventories and highlighting the Nearest 5 Pharmacies with instant Google Maps navigation and digital checkout.

Contact Support

Nearest 5 Pharmacies & Maps

Using the browser Geolocation API and the Haversine spherical distance formula, our platform calculates exact distances between the customer and every registered pharmacy's coordinates (latitude & longitude) and provides one-click Google Maps route navigation.

Live Supabase GRN Stock

Powered by a relational PostgreSQL schema on Supabase (pharmacies, medicines, grn_stock, customer_orders, and sales_transactions). Customers see verified batch numbers, expiry dates, rack locations, and real-time unit quantities.

Receipt Upload & Cashier Sync

Customers can purchase via Card Payment or upload their Bank Transfer Receipt directly to Supabase Storage. The pharmacy POS Cashier reviews the slip and approves it in real time, notifying the customer immediately.
